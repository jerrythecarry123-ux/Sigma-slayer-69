## 1. RESEARCH AND COMPETITOR ANALYSIS

Before making major changes, research the most successful and visually impressive Roblox fishing games.

Pay particular attention to games such as Fisch, Fish It, Fishing Simulator, and other relevant competitors.

Analyze:

- What makes their fishing mechanics satisfying and addictive?
- How do their animations, environments, and visual effects work?
- What UI/UX design patterns are currently popular?
- How do they create excitement when players catch rare fish?
- What progression systems encourage players to return?
- How do they differentiate fish rarity visually?
- What gameplay features are players requesting or criticizing?
- What makes their environments immersive?
- What aspects could be significantly improved?

Research current Roblox game design, animation, visual effects, and gameplay trends using accessible, reliable sources.

Do not blindly copy competitors. Identify their strengths and weaknesses, then design something distinctive that improves upon their best ideas.

If internet access is unavailable, be transparent about that limitation and rely on established Roblox development practices rather than inventing research findings.

## 2. VISUAL QUALITY — EXTREMELY IMPORTANT

Our game must have a recognizable and cohesive visual identity.

I do not want a generic Roblox simulator with basic environments, lifeless animations, and poorly designed interfaces.

Aim for a premium stylized art direction with:

- Beautiful, detailed fishing environments.
- Atmospheric lighting and carefully selected color palettes.
- Visually appealing water with convincing movement.
- Natural environmental animations.
- Dynamic weather and time-of-day effects where appropriate.
- High-quality particle effects.
- Carefully designed fish models with recognizable silhouettes.
- Consistent visual styling throughout the game.
- Smooth camera movement.
- Attractive and polished menus.
- Strong environmental composition and memorable locations.

Use Roblox's available rendering capabilities intelligently.

Consider appropriate use of Atmosphere, ColorCorrection, Bloom, DepthOfField, particle emitters, beams, trails, animations, and lighting effects.

Make environments visually impressive without sacrificing performance on mobile devices or lower-end computers.

Avoid excessive particle spam, harsh bloom, visual clutter, and inconsistent art styles.

**The goal is not simply to add more effects. The goal is to create an intentional, professional-looking game.**

## 3. FISH RARITY SYSTEM

Create a complete, expandable fish rarity system.

Start with the following rarity levels:

1. Common
2. Uncommon
3. Rare
4. Epic
5. Legendary
6. Mythical
7. Secret

Each rarity must feel meaningfully different.

### Visual identity

Common fish should have relatively natural appearances and simple animations.

Uncommon fish can feature more interesting colors and subtle details.

Rare fish should have distinctive patterns, silhouettes, or subtle particle effects.

Epic fish should feel noticeably special through their models, animations, and presentation.

Legendary fish should be visually striking, with distinctive designs, unique visual effects, and memorable catch sequences.

Mythical fish should feel extraordinary, potentially featuring magical or supernatural design elements.

Secret fish should be exceptionally distinctive, mysterious, and memorable.

**IMPORTANT:** Do not differentiate fish solely by changing their colors.

Higher rarity fish should also receive increasingly unique models, silhouettes, animations, behaviors, effects, and presentation.

Create a modular fish database containing information such as:

- Name
- Rarity
- Base value
- Weight range
- Spawn probability
- Habitat
- Environmental requirements
- Model and animation references
- Special attributes
- Catch difficulty
- Visual effect configuration

The system must be easy to expand with additional fish later.

## 4. MAKE FISHING FEEL AMAZING

Fishing should be the central attraction of the game.

Every stage of catching a fish needs to feel responsive, satisfying, and polished.

### Casting

Implement an engaging casting mechanic with:

- Smooth fishing rod animations.
- Natural casting trajectories.
- Responsive controls.
- A satisfying casting strength or accuracy mechanic.
- Convincing fishing line behavior.
- Water splash effects.
- Appropriate sound effects.
- Smooth transitions between fishing states.

### Waiting for a bite

The waiting phase should create anticipation without becoming boring.

Introduce subtle visual and audio cues when fish approach or bite.

Consider fish-specific bite behaviors and occasional environmental interactions.

### Reeling

Design a satisfying reeling minigame involving appropriate player skill.

Possible mechanics include tension management, directional resistance, timing, and fish movement.

Different fish and rarities should behave differently.

Avoid making fishing purely dependent on random chance.

### Catching

Successfully catching a fish should feel rewarding.

Add:

- Responsive rod animations.
- Appropriate camera movement.
- Satisfying sound feedback.
- Water splash effects.
- Fish reveal animations.
- Clear rarity indicators.
- Smooth transitions back into normal gameplay.

Normal catches should be quick and satisfying. Exceptional catches should feel like memorable events.

## 5. CINEMATIC CATCH ANIMATIONS AND CUTSCENES

One of the most important features is the presentation of rare fish.

Create special animations and cinematic sequences for exceptional catches.

### Epic fish

Introduce a brief, polished reveal with attractive effects, appropriate sound, and a distinctive rarity presentation.

### Legendary fish

Create a more dramatic cinematic sequence.

Possible elements include:

- Dynamic camera positioning.
- Smooth camera transitions.
- A dramatic fish reveal.
- A visually impressive fish animation.
- Distinctive lighting and particle effects.
- Brief slow-motion moments.
- A unique sound cue.
- An animated fish name and rarity display.

### Mythical and Secret fish

These should have the most memorable presentations in the game.

Consider fish-specific cinematic variations rather than reusing identical sequences for every catch.

The reveal should create genuine excitement and make the player want to show friends what they caught.

**IMPORTANT REQUIREMENTS:**

- Cutscenes must be polished and not excessively long.
- Players should be able to skip them.
- Repeated animations must not become annoying.
- Cinematics must not interrupt other players unnecessarily.
- Provide reduced-effects options where appropriate.
- Camera control must reliably return to the player afterward.
- Animations must never leave the player stuck or unable to continue playing.

Use smooth easing, properly timed animation events, and carefully designed transitions.

## 6. ANIMATION QUALITY

Review all existing animations and improve anything that appears stiff, abrupt, unnatural, or unfinished.

Focus particularly on:

- Casting animations.
- Rod bending and movement.
- Fish struggling against the line.
- Reeling animations.
- Character reactions.
- Fish movement.
- Fish reveal animations.
- UI transitions.
- Camera animations.
- Environmental movement.

Use suitable interpolation, easing, animation blending, and procedural movement.

Animations should communicate weight, momentum, resistance, and impact.

Different fish should have different personalities through their movement.

For example, a small common fish should not behave identically to a massive legendary fish.

Prioritize responsiveness and consistency rather than adding unnecessary animation complexity.

- Easy to navigate.
- Responsive.

## 8. PROGRESSION AND LONG-TERM GAMEPLAY

Create progression that makes players want to continue fishing because they are discovering something interesting, improving their skills, or reaching meaningful milestones.

Consider implementing or improving:

- Rod upgrades with meaningful differences.
- New fishing locations.
- Fish collections and discovery journals.
- Fish weight variations.
- Rare fish variants.
- Environmental fishing conditions.
- Special fishing events.
- Achievements and milestones.
- Player customization.
- Location-specific fish populations.

Make progression rewarding without turning the game into a repetitive or frustrating grind.

Rare catches should feel exciting because they are special, not simply because their numerical values are higher.

Build a gameplay loop that combines relaxation, exploration, skill, collection, and occasional moments of excitement.
